Noor Field Copier — Grade 10 English (English for Me 10A) — Semester 1

Separate: one Noor form per period with all ten fields; use it when you fill in a separate plan for each period.
Combined: one form for the whole lesson; each field contains all of its periods under “Period 1:”, “Period 2:”; use it when Noor accepts one field per lesson.
Copy each field with its own Copy button, then paste it into the matching field on the Noor platform (fill in one field at a time).
